    Partners

    Trusted by community partners.

    We collaborate with schools, senior communities, and nonprofits to align service with real needs.

    Senior Tech Help

    Confidence with everyday technology.

    We teach practical tech skills that help seniors feel more confident and connected. Our sessions focus on patience, safety, and real-life tasks like calling family, managing settings, and staying in touch.

    Monthly Tech Support

    Our monthly tech support visits serve residents at Five Star Premier Residences, The Palace at Weston, Palomino Gardens, and Wynmoor Village. Volunteers offer patient, one-on-one help with phones, tablets, and computers, from email and video calls to accessibility settings and everyday troubleshooting.

    Mamma’s Curry Closet Christmas Donation

    We came to Mamma’s Curry Closet to donate hundreds of board games for Christmas, helping bring holiday joy and shared play to underserved children.

    Digital Safety Workshops

    We bring digital safety and literacy workshops to Five Star Premier Residences, The Palace at Weston, Palomino Gardens, and Wynmoor Village. Practical lessons help residents recognize scams, avoid phishing, protect personal information, and use everyday technology with more confidence.

    Personalized Tech Assistance

    We provide one-on-one tech support for senior residents at Palomino Gardens and in Plantation. Our volunteers assist with phones, iPads, and laptops, helping with everything from setting up email and video calls to organizing photos and adjusting accessibility settings.

    Youth Programs

    Hands-on learning that sparks curiosity.

    We bring digital safety and literacy workshops to Lotus House, His House Children’s Home, and Mamma Curry’s Closet. Age-appropriate lessons, games, and interactive activities help children build useful digital skills. Our team also collaborates with the American Heritage Game Dev Club on creative learning experiences.

    Friendly Feud Trivia Nights

    We raised $5.6k with a student-led trivia night that brought the community together. Over 200 students participated, forming teams and competing in rounds while enjoying an evening of fun and learning together.

    Game Development Workshops

    Hands-on sessions introducing game design principles and beginner-friendly tools for over 100 participants. Co-led with the American Heritage Game Dev Club to showcase student projects and mentor peers.

    Bake Sales

    Students planned, baked, and ran the table to support our programs for kids and seniors. These pop-up sales build teamwork and show how small efforts add up.

    Broward Health Center Visit

    In partnership with the Game Dev Club, we visited Broward Health Center to play board games with pediatric patients. Our volunteers spent time with kids and their families, creating moments of joy and connection through interactive games that brought smiles and laughter to the facility.

    Tech Donations

    Putting useful tools into the right hands.

    From shared iPads and school supplies to books, board games, and period products, our donation drives put useful resources into the hands of children and families. We also support communities in Peru with computers, school and art supplies, and sports equipment.

    Mamma’s Curry Christmas Celebration

    Partnering with Mamma’s Curry for a special Christmas celebration, we spent time with kids playing board games and spreading holiday cheer. Our volunteers brought festive joy through interactive games and activities, making the holiday season memorable with laughter, connection, and the spirit of giving.

    His House Children’s Home iPad Donation

    We donated 15+ iPads to His House Children’s Home to expand shared technology access for the children. This includes seven iPads with chargers and cases supported by a $950 Karma for Cara microgrant. Our volunteers also taught digital literacy, device use, and financial literacy skills to help children make practical use of the technology.

    Miami Chapter: Lotus House Donation Drive

    Our Miami chapter donated board games, period products, and books to Lotus House, supporting children and families with everyday essentials and opportunities to read and play.

    Computers, Supplies, and Sports Equipment for Peru

    Our outreach in Peru includes donated computers, school and art supplies, and sports equipment, supporting classroom learning, creativity, and play.

    School Supplies for Boys & Girls Clubs

    We donated school supplies to Boys & Girls Clubs to support children’s learning and access to classroom essentials.

    Board Games for His House and Lotus House

    Our board game drives support His House Children’s Home and Lotus House with shared activities that encourage connection, teamwork, and screen-free play.

    Karma for Cara Microgrant

    A $950 microgrant from the Karma for Cara Foundation helped fund seven iPads, chargers, and cases for His House Children’s Home. The iPads are shared among the children for everyday learning and digital access, supported by our digital literacy education.

    Christmas Event: Ongoing Haiti Initiative

    Last Christmas, Bytes for Better Lives donated board games to a rural school in Haiti, spreading holiday joy while providing children with games they can enjoy together and use to practice learning English through play. We are grateful to House of New Vision and Hope for making this Christmas celebration possible and look forward to continuing to spread connection through board games!

    Nicklaus Children’s Hospital

    We donated board games to Nicklaus Children’s Hospital. These games provide young patients with engaging activities that bring comfort, joy, and distraction during their hospital stays and recovery.

    His House Children’s Home

    We donated board games to His House Children’s Home. These games provide children with screen-free activities that encourage social connection, creativity, and meaningful interactions during their time at the facility.

    Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital

    We organized board game drives and donated games to Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital. These games provide children with engaging activities that bring comfort, distraction, and a sense of normalcy during their hospital stays.

    Mamma’s Curry Closet

    We donated board games to Mamma’s Curry Closet in our local community. These games provide children and families with engaging activities that bring joy and create meaningful moments together in their community space.

    Haiti Support Initiative

    We donated games to the non-profit House of New Vision and Hope, which supports disadvantaged children in rural Haiti. We provided chess, checkers, and card games that don’t require English proficiency, making them accessible and enjoyable for all children.
