IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS:
--------------------
- Format: JPG for photos, PNG for logos with transparency
- Team photos: 800x800px (square, centered headshots)
- Event photos: 1200x800px (landscape orientation)
- Partner logos: 400x200px (transparent background recommended)
- Hero images: 1920x1080px (high resolution for large displays)

UPDATING THE HTML:
-----------------
Once you add images, update the HTML files:

1. For team photos in team.html:
   Replace: <div class="team-photo">👨‍💼</div>
   With: <img src="images/team-pranav.jpg" alt="Pranav Rai" class="team-photo">

2. For card icons:
   Replace: <div class="card-icon">🎲</div>
   With: <img src="images/event-board-game.jpg" alt="Board Game Drive" class="card-icon">

3. For partner logos in impact.html:
   Add logo images to the partner boxes
