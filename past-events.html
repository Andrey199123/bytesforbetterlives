EVENTS

Our journey of making a difference in the community

September Board Game Drive

Thanks to the American Heritage community, Bytes for Better Lives collected over 350 board games, from Uno and Jenga to Chess, Connect Four, and Monopoly. We are thankful to have over 70 amazing students to donate games.

Lotus House

Bytes for Better Lives helps bridge the digital divide at the Lotus House through our digital safety and financial literacy presentation. We taught women in need how to navigate the digital world safely and provided information on financial literacy. We held a board game raffle and provided snacks to make it a truly memorable experience.

Digital Safety

We held digital safety lectures for senior citizens, teaching them how to prevent scams and stay safe while navigating the digital world. We held these lectures at the 5 Star Premier Residencies and the Palace at Weston.

Personalized Tech Assistance

Bytes for Better Lives helps bridge the digital divide at Palomino Gardens by offering patient, one-on-one tech support for senior residents. Our volunteers assist with phones, iPads, and laptops, everything from setting up email and video calls to organizing photos, adjusting accessibility settings, and spotting online scams.

Board Game Donations - His House Children's Home

In July 2025, we donated over 70 board games to His House Children's Home. Our goal was to give the children fun and screen-free activities that encourage social connection and joy.

Board Game Drive - Joe DiMaggio's Children Hospital

We organized a few Board Game Drives and amassed over 200 board and card games, which we donated to Joe DiMaggio Children's Hospital. Our mission was to provide kids with engaging activities that bring comfort, distraction, and a feeling of joy during their hospital stay.

Mamma's Curry Closet - Board Game Donation

We donated 100+ board games to Mamma's Curry Closet, providing children and families with fun, engaging activities that bring joy and create meaningful moments together.

Friendly Feud Trivia Nights

We have hosted two Trivia Nights at American Heritage School and raised over $2,500. The event brought over 120 students together to compete in teams and spectate, combining endless fun and laughter with education, while also supporting a meaningful cause.

Tech Workshops

We held a series of lectures and workshops on topics like game development and how to create your own game, reaching an audience of over 50 people. These events aimed to inspire creativity, teach valuable skills, and make game design more accessible to everyone.

Our Support in Haiti

We donated a portion of the games we collected to the non-profit House of New Vision and Hope. This non-profit works towards improving the lives of disadvantaged children in rural regions in Haiti. The games we donated were chess, checkers, and card games, which don't require English proficiency to learn, making it easier for children to enjoy!
