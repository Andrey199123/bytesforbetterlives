## 📸 How to Use

### Carousel Navigation:
1. Click the ◀ ▶ arrows on the image
2. Click the dots at the bottom to jump to any image
3. Hover over carousel and use keyboard arrows

### Fullscreen Viewing:
1. **Click any image** in the carousel
2. Lightbox opens in fullscreen
3. Use ◀ ▶ or keyboard arrows to navigate all images
4. Press **ESC** or click **X** to close
5. Click outside the image to close
