Student-led nonprofit in South Florida

    Bridging the digital divide for seniors and children.

    Bytes for Better Lives provides senior tech help, digital literacy education, online safety presentations, and donations that support children, families, and community partners.

    What We Do

    Technology access with patience, safety, and follow-through.

    BFBL combines direct service with education: students help seniors feel more confident with technology, support children through donations and learning, and build chapters that can serve new communities with clear standards.

    Leadership

    Board of Directors

    The board of directors guides BFBL programs, partnerships, chapter growth, and student volunteer coordination. Founded by Aaron Pinto and Pranav Rai.

    Chapters

    A growing service model with chapters across communities.

    Nine chapters — from South Florida to New York, Virginia, and abroad — run senior tech help, digital-literacy lessons, and donation drives in their own communities.
