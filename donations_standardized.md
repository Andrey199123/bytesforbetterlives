## Key Standardization Elements

### Common Patterns:
1. **Quantity mentioned first**: "over 350", "over 70", "over 200", "over 100"
2. **Recipient named**: Specific organization or facility
3. **Time/context included**: "in July 2025", "from American Heritage", "in our local community"
4. **Second sentence starts with**: "These games provide..."
5. **Beneficiaries specified**: "children", "children and families", "kids"
6. **Impact described**: Activities, benefits, emotional outcomes

### Consistent Vocabulary:
- "donated" / "collected"
- "over [#] board games"
- "provide [beneficiaries] with"
- "activities that [benefit]"
- "encourage/bring [positive outcome]"
