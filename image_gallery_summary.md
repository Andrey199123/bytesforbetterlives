## 🎯 Features

### Image Gallery
- **Grid Layout**: Responsive grid that adapts to screen size
- **Hover Effects**: Images zoom slightly and show magnifying glass icon on hover
- **Aspect Ratio**: All images maintain 16:9 aspect ratio for consistency
- **Professional Styling**: Rounded corners, smooth transitions

### Lightbox Modal (Just like Wix!)
✅ **Click to Open**: Click any image to open fullscreen lightbox
✅ **Navigation**:
  - Click ◀ ▶ arrows to go previous/next
  - Use keyboard: ← → arrow keys
  - Press ESC to close
  - Click X button to close
  - Click outside image to close

✅ **Image Counter**: Shows "3 / 5" to indicate position in gallery
✅ **Video Support**: Videos play with controls in lightbox
✅ **Fullscreen View**: Images fill the screen for maximum visibility
✅ **Mobile Responsive**: Optimized for touch devices

## 📱 How to Use

### For Users Viewing the Website:
1. Scroll to any event section
2. Click on any image thumbnail
3. Lightbox opens in fullscreen
4. Use arrows or keyboard to navigate through images
5. Press ESC or click X to close

### For Administrators:
Images are automatically ordered by their filename numbers (1, 2, 3, etc.)
