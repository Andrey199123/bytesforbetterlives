Our Donations

    We organize drives and events that put useful items directly into the hands of kids and seniors. Each effort is planned with community partners so donations match real needs.

    Christmas Event: Ongoing Haiti Initiative

    This Christmas, Bytes for Better Lives donated board games to a rural school in Haiti, spreading holiday joy while providing children with games they can enjoy together and use to practice learning English through play. We are grateful to House of New Vision and Hope for making this Christmas celebration possible and look forward to continuing to spread connection through board games!

    Nicklaus Children's Hospital

    We donated board games to Nicklaus Children's Hospital. These games provide young patients with engaging activities that bring comfort, joy, and distraction during their hospital stays and recovery.

    His House Children's Home

    We donated board games to His House Children's Home. These games provide children with screen-free activities that encourage social connection, creativity, and meaningful interactions during their time at the facility.

    Joe DiMaggio Children's Hospital

    We organized board game drives and donated games to Joe DiMaggio Children's Hospital. These games provide children with engaging activities that bring comfort, distraction, and a sense of normalcy during their hospital stays.

    Mamma's Curry Closet

    We donated board games to Mamma's Curry Closet in our local community. These games provide children and families with engaging activities that bring joy and create meaningful moments together in their community space.

    Haiti Support Initiative

    We donated games to the non-profit House of New Vision and Hope, which supports disadvantaged children in rural Haiti. We provided chess, checkers, and card games that don't require English proficiency, making them accessible and enjoyable for all children.
