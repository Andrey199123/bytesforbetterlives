Ongoing Initiatives

We maintain long-term programs that create sustained impact through regular visits and continuous support. These recurring initiatives build lasting relationships and provide ongoing resources to communities we serve.

Educational Technologies

We are actively raising funds to donate iPads to His House Children's Home. Our volunteers teach weekly interactive programming lessons, ensuring children can follow along and engage with modern technology in a supportive learning environment.

Recurring Senior Visits

We return to senior communities each month to offer personalized tech help. Locations include places like Five Star Premier, with sessions coordinated around resident needs.
